I start a lot of projects. Some ship, most stall, and a few turn into something I'm proud of. Up to now they've lived in scattered repos and half-finished notes.

This blog is the fix. For each project I want to write down:

- **What** I was trying to build, and why
- **The decisions** I made along the way, and the alternatives I passed on
- **What broke**, because that's usually where the learning is
- **What I'd do differently** next time

## How this site works

It's an [Astro](https://astro.build) site. Posts are Markdown files in `src/content/blog/`, the HTML is static, and almost no JavaScript ships to your browser.

Try the **Aa** button up top to switch typefaces, and the sun/moon to switch themes.
